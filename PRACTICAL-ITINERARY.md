# Europe 2026 — Practical Day-by-Day Itinerary

**15 Jun → 6 Jul 2026 · 3 travellers (Berlin week is the bigger company group) · cook-in plan**

This rebuilds the trip around six real constraints:

1. **Check-in / check-out times** of each booked apartment.
2. **Self-check-in vs. meeting a host** — when you have a flexible code, and when you must "hop in" on time.
3. **Full days, 06:30–07:00 out → ~18:00–19:00 back**, then cook.
4. **Cooking in the apartments** → grocery runs, and *when shops are open*.
5. **Days something closes** — Sundays (DACH shops shut), Monday museum closures, religious closures.
6. **Best vs. worst (overcrowded) time** to hit each sight.

> **How to read each day:** 🔑 logistics · 🌅/☀️/🌆 morning/afternoon/evening · 🍳 cook & groceries · ⚠️ watch-out.

## 🛒 Grocery & Sunday matrix (the cook-in plan lives or dies here)

European shop rules by country:

- 🇮🇹 **Italy** (Conad, Carrefour Express, Coop) — ~08:00–20:00; many open Sun mornings. Easy.
- 🇨🇭 **Switzerland** (Coop, Migros) — weekdays to ~19:00, Sat to ~17:00, **closed Sun** — *except station Coop/Migros (Luzern, Bern Hbf) open Sun ~07:00–22:00*. Cooking here saves the most money.
- 🇦🇹 **Austria** (Billa, Spar, Hofer) — to ~19:30/20:00, Sat to ~18:00, **closed Sun** — *except Hbf Billa/Spar (Wien, Salzburg, Innsbruck) open Sun*.
- 🇨🇿 **Czechia** (Albert, Billa, Lidl) — open daily incl. Sun, ~07:00–22:00. Easy.
- 🇩🇪 **Germany** (REWE, Edeka, Lidl, Aldi) — to ~20:00–22:00, **closed Sun** — *except **REWE in Berlin Hbf, open Sun 08:00–22:00*** + Spätis.
- 🇳🇱 **Netherlands** (Albert Heijn, Jumbo) — open daily incl. Sun. Easy.

**Your three problem Sundays:**

| Sunday | Where you are | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun 21 Jun** | Bern → Lauterach/Bregenz (CH→AT) | Shops shut both sides. **Buy Sun dinner + Mon breakfast/train food at Bern Hbf Coop/Migros before you leave** (open Sun). In Bregenz: eat the lakeside dinner *out*; restock Mon AM at Bregenz station. |
| **Sun 28 Jun** | Prague → Berlin (CZ→DE) | You arrive **Berlin Hbf 15:00 → shop at the Hbf REWE on arrival (open Sun 08–22)** before heading to the flat. Stock for Mon too. |
| **Sun 5 Jul** | Amsterdam day-trip | NL open Sundays — no problem (Albert Heijn). |

**Recurring cook-in rhythm:** the evening you arrive (or at the arrival station), buy *dinner + next-day breakfast + a train lunch*. Cook dinner, pack the lunch. Repeat. Skip cooking only on 1-night stops with a dawn exit (Como) — there, buy grab-and-go the night before.
