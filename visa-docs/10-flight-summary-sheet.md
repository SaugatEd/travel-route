## Sponsor payment confirmation — what to attach

Pick **one** of the following (or include all if available):

- (a) Turkish Airlines invoice addressed to **eduneon GmbH** for EUR 3,826.59 under PNR TCA424, dated 10 April 2026
- (b) Corporate-card receipt from eduneon's payment processor showing the EUR 3,826.59 charge to Turkish Airlines
- (c) Single-line bank-statement extract from eduneon's bank showing the outgoing payment to Turkish Airlines
- (d) A short letter on eduneon letterhead, signed and stamped, stating
  *"We confirm that the total fare of EUR 3,826.59 under PNR TCA424 was paid in full by eduneon GmbH on [date], invoice attached."*

> The flight e-ticket on its own does **not** prove the sponsor paid. The visa officer wants a *separate* item proving payment, on top of the e-ticket itself.
