## Why this is a *separate* letter

- The visa officer reads the **Employment Verification Letter** for the *fact of employment*.
- The visa officer reads the **Leave Approval Letter** for the *fact of return*.
- Two short, single-purpose letters are stronger than one combined letter that mixes the two.
- Two letters also signal that the employer has a **formal HR process** — a strong positive credibility signal.

## Date consistency (cross-check before printing)

- [ ] Leave **start** ≥ 1 calendar day **before** the outbound flight (16 Jun) — recommended 15 Jun
- [ ] Leave **end** ≥ 3 working days **after** the return flight (7 Jul early Delhi arrival) — recommended 10 Jul
- [ ] Resumption-of-duty date is on a **working day** (Monday 13 Jul 2026 is ideal)
- [ ] These dates match the Cover Letter (Tab 6) and the Itinerary (Tab 12)
- [ ] The Employment Verification Letter (Tab 16) **also** carries the same dates — both letters consistent
