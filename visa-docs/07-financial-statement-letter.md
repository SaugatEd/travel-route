## Why this letter exists

- It **prevents** the visa officer from having to dig through the bank statement to understand the applicant's finances.
- It **explains** any large deposit *before* it becomes a question.
- It **states the source** of funds in plain English — pre-empts the "where did this money come from?" line of questioning.
- It **declares no borrowed funds** — a clean answer to one of the most common refusal triggers.

## Documents this letter cross-references (must all be present)

- **Tab 19** — Six-month personal bank statement, bank-stamped on every page
- **Tab 19a** — Bonus letter or other documentary support for the first large deposit
- **Tab 19b** — Fixed-deposit closure receipt
- **Tab 19c** — Wise statement / counterparty bank statement
- **Tab 19d** — Credit-card statement
- **Tab 4** — Passport last page with FX endorsement (if applicable)
