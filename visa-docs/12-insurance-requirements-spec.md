# Travel Insurance — Specification & Order Sheet

> **Use this document to:** (a) brief the Nepali insurance broker / agent on exactly what to issue; (b) verify the certificate before submitting it at Tab 15. **Do not submit this internal spec sheet to the embassy** — only the certificate itself goes in the file.

---

## Hard requirements (Schengen Visa Code Article 15)

| Requirement | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| **Minimum medical coverage** | **EUR 30,000** (target EUR 50,000 for stronger application) |
| **Geographic scope** | **"All Schengen countries"** OR an explicit list including all 6 trip countries (DE, IT, CH, AT, CZ, NL) |
| **Validity start** | **on or before 16 June 2026** (recommend 15 Jun 2026 for buffer) |
| **Validity end** | **on or after 7 July 2026** (recommend 8 Jul 2026 for buffer) |
| **Total days of cover** | **≥ 23 days** (trip is 21 days but buy 1-day buffer on each side) |
| **Medical repatriation** | **Required** — to country of residence |
| **Repatriation of remains** | **Required** |
| **Emergency medical evacuation** | **Required** |
| **In-patient hospital treatment** | **Required** |
| **24/7 emergency assistance helpline** | **Required** |
| **COVID-19 / pandemic treatment** | **Recommended** (most modern policies include this) |
| **Deductible / excess** | **Recommended: zero deductible**; if any, ≤ EUR 100 |
| **Currency of cover** | **EUR** (not USD or NPR — the embassy wants EUR figures) |
| **Issuer authorisation** | Authorised in a Schengen state OR a Nepali insurer accepted by the German Embassy |
| **Language of certificate** | **English** (Nepali-only certificate will be rejected) |
| **Format** | On insurer letterhead, with policy number, named insured, dates, scope, limits, signature/seal |

```

   Number of insured travellers   :  [N — typically 1, 2, or 3]
   Names of insured (per passport):  [Names]
   Passport numbers               :  [XXXX]
   Date of birth of each insured  :  [DOBs]

   Coverage start date            :  15 June 2026 (one day before flight)
   Coverage end date              :   8 July 2026 (one day after return arrival)
   Total days                     :  24

   Geographic scope               :  All Schengen states
                                     (must explicitly say "All Schengen countries"
                                      OR list:  DE, IT, CH, AT, CZ, NL, plus the

   Medical coverage limit         :  Minimum EUR 30,000  -  please quote also for
                                     EUR 50,000 and EUR 100,000 tiers.

     - In-patient hospital treatment
     - Out-patient medical treatment
     - Emergency medical evacuation
     - Medical repatriation to Nepal
     - Repatriation of remains
     - 24x7 emergency assistance helpline

   Required certificate format    :  English, insurer letterhead, with policy
                                     number, named insured, validity dates,
                                     geographic scope ("All Schengen countries"),
                                     coverage limit in EUR, and benefits summary.
```

## Common rejection causes (insurance) — to check before paying

- ❌ Coverage **less than EUR 30,000** — automatic refusal
- ❌ Geographic scope says only "Germany" — must say **all Schengen**
- ❌ Validity ends **on** the return date at midnight — buy 1 day buffer
- ❌ Certificate **in Nepali only** — must be English (or English translation)
- ❌ Issuer is not Schengen-authorised and not on the German Embassy's accepted-insurer list
- ❌ Certificate is a **quote** or **proposal**, not a **policy** — make sure it is the final policy document
- ❌ Policy excludes "tourism" or "leisure travel" — read the exclusions
- ❌ Repatriation of remains **not** explicitly covered
- ❌ Certificate signed only by the broker, not by the insurer — must be insurer letterhead
- ❌ Deductible higher than EUR 100 — read the schedule

## Estimated premium (informational, for budget)

| Tier | Days | Premium per person (NPR) | Premium per person (EUR) |
|------|------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| EUR 30,000 | 24 | NPR 4,500 – 6,500 | EUR 30 – 45 |
| EUR 50,000 | 24 | NPR 6,500 – 8,500 | EUR 45 – 60 |
| EUR 100,000 | 24 | NPR 9,000 – 12,000 | EUR 60 – 85 |

> **Recommendation:** Buy the **EUR 50,000 tier**. The marginal cost (NPR 2,000) is trivial relative to the application cost, and the higher coverage is a small but positive signal to the visa officer.

## What to do with the certificate after issue

1. Print 2 copies: one for Tab 15 of the embassy file, one for your personal travel folder
2. Carry the **policy number + 24/7 helpline** on a card in your wallet during the trip
3. Save the PDF to your phone (offline) and email it to a family member as a backup
