## Mandatory elements the visa officer will physically check

- [ ] **Original eduneon letterhead** (printed by the company, not a Word reproduction)
- [ ] **Wet-ink blue signature** — visibly not photocopied
- [ ] **Company stamp / seal** physically overlapping the signature
- [ ] **HRB number** present in the letterhead **and** in the body — verifiable on `handelsregister.de`
- [ ] **VAT ID (USt-IdNr.)** present — verifiable on the EU VIES portal
- [ ] **Real German postal address** of eduneon, not a virtual-office address if at all avoidable
- [ ] **Signatory's direct phone and email** — not just a generic `info@`
- [ ] **Dates of invitation (28 Jun – 3 Jul 2026)** match the applicant's visa application dates and the itinerary at Tab 12
- [ ] **PNR TCA424 and fare EUR 3,826.59** match the flight booking at Tab 11
- [ ] **The bounded language** "the sponsorship is strictly limited to items (a), (b), and (c)" must remain — this is the most important clause
- [ ] **Letter dated ≤ 90 days** before the embassy appointment

## Common rejection causes specific to invitation letters (to neutralise)

- ❌ Letter exceeds 90 days at appointment date — **request a fresh one**
- ❌ Generic `Dear Sir/Madam, please grant a visa to our employee` — too vague — **use this template's bounded language**
- ❌ "All expenses will be borne by the company" — **never say this** unless literally true (and it isn't here); the bounded language must be exact
- ❌ Signatory's name on letter ≠ signatory of the wet signature
- ❌ Stamp missing or placed away from the signature
- ❌ Sponsor's HRB unverifiable — verify on `handelsregister.de` before printing
