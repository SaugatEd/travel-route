## Section C — Employer-issued letters that accompany this summary

The following two letters are produced separately by the employer on its own letterhead and bound directly after this summary in the physical file:

1. **Employment Confirmation Letter** — Tab 16
   - Issued on employer letterhead, addressed to the German Embassy.
   - States: position, joining date, current monthly salary, employment type.
   - Signed by HR Head or Director, stamped with company seal.

2. **Leave Approval Letter** — Tab 17 (separate from the above)
   - Issued on employer letterhead, addressed to the German Embassy.
   - States explicit leave dates (15 June 2026 → 10 July 2026) and return-to-work date (13 July 2026).
   - States that the position is held during the leave period.
   - Signed by HR Head or Director, stamped with company seal.

> Templates for both letters are at `visa-docs/05-employment-letter-template.md` and `visa-docs/06-leave-approval-letter.md` of this folder. Provide them to your employer for issuance on letterhead.
