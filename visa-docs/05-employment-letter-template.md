## Verification checklist (employer should re-read before signing)

- [ ] Letter on **physical** company letterhead, not a Word reproduction
- [ ] Reference number assigned and recorded in HR register
- [ ] Date is within **30 days** of the embassy appointment
- [ ] Employee's name **matches the passport exactly**
- [ ] Passport number cross-checked
- [ ] Joining date matches the employee's personnel record
- [ ] Salary amount matches the **latest salary slip**
- [ ] Leave dates **cover the entire trip plus a 3–5 day buffer** after the return flight (recommend `[16 Jun 2026]` → `[10 Jul 2026]` minimum)
- [ ] Resumption-of-duty date is on a **working day**, with a buffer after return flight (7 Jul early morning Delhi arrival → recommend back to work on or after 13 Jul)
- [ ] Direct phone of HR signatory works and goes to a person, not voicemail
- [ ] Wet-ink **blue** signature
- [ ] Company stamp/seal physically overlaps the signature
- [ ] Two-language preference: English; if Nepali version also issued, attach translation

## What strengthens this letter further

- A **printed organisation chart** showing the applicant in their team (one page)
- A **service letter** in addition to this verification, if the applicant has long tenure
- A copy of the **appointment letter** when the applicant joined (Tab 16b)
- **Statutory contributions** (PF/SSF/CIT) printout showing ongoing membership beyond the trip period
