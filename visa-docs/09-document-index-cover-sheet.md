## How to physically prepare the cover sheet

- **A4 paper, 80 gsm white**, portrait orientation
- Sits at the very top of the loose-page file (before Tab 1)
- Use **a clear plastic L-folder** for the whole submission — the cover sheet shows through the plastic front
- Do not staple the cover sheet to anything — it sits alone
- If you have multiple applicants in the same family, **prepare one cover sheet per applicant** — files are submitted individually even when applied together
